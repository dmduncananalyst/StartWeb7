I NEED MORE MONTHLY SECURITY CONTRACTS. WHAT SHOULD I DO?

To win more monthly security contracts, target properties your team can serve reliably, contact the people who arrange coverage, and follow up with clear proposals. Track which conversations become signed, profitable agreements.

Choose the contracts you want

List the property types, locations, hours, and coverage your team can reliably serve. A property manager seeking overnight coverage has a different buying process from an event organizer needing a single shift. Focus your outreach on recurring work that fits your staffing and service area.

Reach the people who arrange security

Make a list of local property managers, facility directors, building owners, and businesses that need regular coverage. Ask existing clients and professional contacts for introductions, and follow up with a short description of the service you can provide. Keep track of who you contacted and when to follow up.

Make the next step easy

When someone expresses interest, ask about the site, schedule, responsibilities, and intended start date. Offer a site visit when appropriate, then provide a clear proposal that explains the coverage, reporting, contact process, and monthly price. Respond promptly to questions so a promising conversation does not stall.

Help buyers check your fit

Your website can confirm the services, property types, and areas you cover, and give buyers a direct way to contact you. Search visibility can help new prospects discover the company, but it is only one source of opportunities alongside introductions, outreach, and repeat business.

Track signed contracts

Record each lead source, site visit, proposal, and signed monthly agreement. Review which property types and outreach methods produce work your team can serve profitably. Use that information to decide where to spend your next month of effort.
