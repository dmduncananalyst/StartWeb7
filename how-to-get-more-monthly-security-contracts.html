SECURITY SERVICES

I NEED MORE MONTHLY SECURITY CONTRACTS. WHAT SHOULD I DO?

Start by making it clear who you protect, which areas you cover, and what ongoing security service looks like. Decision makers need a fast, trustworthy way to understand whether your company is the right fit.

Make your service offer easy to understand

A property manager or business owner should not have to guess what you do. Give each important service a clear page, explain the locations and properties you protect, and show how a potential client can request information about monthly coverage.

Make your website work beyond referrals

Referrals can be valuable, but a website gives businesses another way to discover you while they are actively looking. SEO, Search Engine Optimization, helps your security company appear in Google searches for the security services you provide and the markets you serve.

Answer the questions clients ask before they contact you

Write clear answers about your service areas, coverage types, contract process, and the types of properties you work with. This helps a visitor feel prepared to reach out and gives search tools useful information about your company.

What is AEO?

AEO, Answer Engine Optimization, helps make your website easier for AI search tools to understand, giving your business more opportunities to appear in AI answer boxes and responses when people ask questions in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

Measure what becomes a contract

Keep track of where each serious inquiry came from, which conversations become meetings, and which proposals become ongoing contracts. That gives you a clearer picture of where to keep investing time and budget.
