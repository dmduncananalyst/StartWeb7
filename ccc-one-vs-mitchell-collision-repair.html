Collision Repair Marketing: How to Get More Leads

Collision repair shops typically get new business through insurance company referrals, repeat customers, reviews, and increasingly, their own website, each reaching a driver at a different point after their accident.

Insurance company referral networks

Can generate steady work, but a shop has limited control over this source, and it depends on maintaining that specific insurer relationship.

Reviews and reputation

A strong set of Google reviews matters significantly in this industry, since most drivers have never needed a collision repair shop before and are choosing somewhat blind after an accident. Reviews build the trust needed to convert someone who's already looking, but they don't put a shop in front of a driver who hasn't found them yet.

Repair management software

Platforms like CCC ONE and Mitchell help manage the estimating and repair process once a vehicle is already in the shop, tracking parts, insurer communication, and job progress. These tools genuinely improve how a shop operates, but they don't do anything to bring a new customer through the door in the first place, that part of the process happens entirely before either platform is involved.

How StartWeb7 Gets Your Business More Leads

SEARCH

They may also open an AI platform, such as ChatGPT or Claude, and ask the question there. They could ask about a service they need or information connected to that service. For example, they might type "Will insurance pay for collision repair?"

CLICK

LEAD

POTENTIAL CUSTOMER

You follow up with the lead and have the opportunity to turn that person into a paying customer.

How this compares to waiting on insurance referrals alone

A website built this way doesn't replace an insurance referral network, but it captures a driver at the earliest possible point, right when they're searching for answers, rather than waiting for an insurer to make the introduction. When a driver requests an estimate from that page, it becomes a lead the shop can act on immediately.

Why this actually works: SEO and AEO

When a driver types a question into Google, Google looks through millions of websites and picks a few to show first. SEO is making sure your website is one of the ones Google picks, by having the actual answer to that question written clearly on your page.

AEO is the same idea, but for AI tools like ChatGPT. Instead of showing a list of websites, the AI reads your page and just tells the person the answer itself. For that to happen, your page needs to have a clear, direct answer the AI can pull from.

This is the real difference from CCC ONE or Mitchell. Those platforms manage a repair once a car is already in your shop, they don't bring the driver to you in the first place. SEO and AEO work earlier than that, they put your shop in front of the driver while they're still searching for answers, before they've chosen anyone.
