COLLISION REPAIR MARKETING

After an accident, drivers look for a repair shop they can trust. Your business needs to be easy to find when that search begins.

The Customer Problem

A driver may need answers quickly after an accident. By the time they choose a repair shop, they have often already searched for information, local options, and reassurance that the shop can help.

How an Owned Lead Source Helps

A collision repair website supported by ongoing SEO + AEO management gives a shop a stronger opportunity to appear while drivers are looking for help.

What Ongoing Management Supports

Ongoing management supports the website, search presence, and content built around the questions drivers ask. The goal is to give the shop a direct path from search to inquiry.

The Bottom Line

Collision repair marketing should help drivers find your shop at the moment they need it—not leave your business dependent on someone else to send the work.
