COLLISION REPAIR MARKETING

Collision repair marketing starts with being easy to find, easy to trust, and easy to contact after an accident. Give drivers and referral partners the information they need, then measure which sources lead to actual repair estimates and jobs.

Help drivers find the shop

Keep business hours, location, phone number, and repair services accurate wherever customers look. A useful website and current local listings can help a driver see whether your shop handles the repair they need.

Show how the repair process works

Explain how to request an estimate, what information to bring, and how customers can discuss scheduling and insurance questions with your team. Avoid making promises about timing or coverage before inspecting the vehicle and confirming the details.

Earn trust before the call

Show the team, facility, relevant qualifications, and genuine customer feedback where available. Give visitors a direct phone or inquiry path that works well on a mobile phone.

Build and measure referral sources

Stay connected with past customers and relevant local partners. Track calls, estimate requests, completed repairs, and the source of each job. That tells you whether a marketing channel creates real work for the shop.
