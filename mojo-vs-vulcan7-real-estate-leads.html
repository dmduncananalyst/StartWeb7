REAL ESTATE

HOW TO GET REAL ESTATE LEADS

To get real estate inquiries, stay in touch with past clients, build local referral relationships, reach prospective buyers and sellers, and make it easy to contact you online. Track which sources lead to appointments and clients so you know where to focus.

Get More CustomersGet Your Instant Audit

Start with the market you serve

Define the neighborhoods, property types, and kinds of clients you know well. That gives your outreach a clear focus and helps you answer the questions local buyers and sellers actually have.

Build referral and outreach habits

Stay in touch with past clients and local partners. For prospecting tools, judge whether the contacts fit your market and whether your team can follow up well. No list becomes a client relationship on its own.

Make it easy to find and contact you

Show your service areas and useful local information on your website. Give visitors a clear way to request a conversation. Search can create another path to your business over time, alongside introductions and direct outreach.

Measure appointments and clients

Record the source of each inquiry, whether it led to a conversation or appointment, and whether it became a client. Compare the time and cost of each channel to decide where to focus next.
