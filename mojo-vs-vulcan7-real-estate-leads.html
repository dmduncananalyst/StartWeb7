How to Get Real Estate Leads

Generating real estate leads usually comes down to a mix of methods, referrals, open houses, social media, paid lead platforms, and a website, each with a different cost and a different level of effort required to turn a name into an actual client.

Referrals

Reliable, but inconsistent, an agent can't control when the next one comes in.

Open houses and in,person networking

Open houses put an agent directly in front of active buyers, and networking events, community involvement, and local sponsorships build the kind of visibility that turns into word,of,mouth leads over time. These methods build a reputation, but they take months or years to compound into a steady lead flow.

Paid lead platforms

Services like Vulcan7 and Mojo sell access to lead lists, expired listings, FSBOs, absentee owners, that agents then cold call. These can work for agents who are comfortable with high call volume, but they cost $89,359+ a month, and every single contact starts as a cold call to someone who hasn't expressed interest in working with that specific agent.

Social media

Instagram and Facebook can build an agent's following and local reputation over time, but they depend on catching the right person at the right moment while scrolling, not while actively searching for real estate help.

How StartWeb7 Gets Your Business More Leads

SEARCH

Someone opens the internet browser on their phone or computer and types a question into the Google search bar. They may also open an AI platform, such as ChatGPT or Claude, and ask the question there. They could ask about a service they need or information connected to that service. For example, they might type "What is an FHA loan?"

CLICK

Your business appears in the results. The person clicks and enters your website.

LEAD

The person calls your business or completes your form. Their information can be sent directly to your CRM.

POTENTIAL CUSTOMER

You follow up with the lead and have the opportunity to turn that person into a paying customer.

How this compares to paying for a lead list

A website built this way doesn't replace referrals or networking, but it changes the economics of the other paid options. Instead of paying monthly for a list of strangers to call, the traffic arrives already self,selected, someone who typed out a specific question because they're actively in the process of buying or selling. That inquiry can be routed into the same CRM already tracking leads from any other source.

Why this actually works: SEO and AEO

When someone types a question into Google, Google looks through millions of websites and picks a few to show first. SEO is making sure your website is one of the ones Google picks, by having the actual answer to that question written clearly on your page.

AEO is the same idea, but for AI tools like ChatGPT. Instead of showing a list of websites, the AI reads your page and just tells the person the answer itself. For that to happen, your page needs to have a clear, direct answer the AI can pull from.

This is the real difference from Vulcan7 or Mojo. Those platforms hand you a list of names and phone numbers, people who haven't said they want to talk to you, you're the one reaching out cold. SEO and AEO work the opposite way: they bring you people who are already searching for exactly what you offer, and they're the ones reaching out to you.
