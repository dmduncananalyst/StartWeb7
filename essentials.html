Essentials SEO and AEO Management

Getting found is essential to bringing in business. Essentials provides ongoing SEO and AEO management to help potential customers find your company when they search for the services you offer.

Customers Need a Way to Find You

A person can need exactly what your business provides without knowing your name. They might search for collision repair near their home, a dentist in their neighborhood or a company to clean their building. Appearing in that research gives your business an opportunity to reach someone beyond its existing customers.

That is the basic purpose of SEO, search engine optimization. It helps your website participate in relevant search results so people looking for your services have a way to encounter your company.

What Getting Found Looks Like

Getting found can mean someone sees your website in Google or Bing, opens it and contacts your business about a service. For local companies, people may also find the business through Google Maps and its Google Business Profile.

These are different places where a potential customer can begin looking. A website visit, a profile view and a phone inquiry are different actions, so getting found is the beginning of a customer relationship rather than a completed sale.

Search Also Happens Through AI

Some people research a service by asking an AI assistant such as ChatGPT. They may ask about a problem before searching for a particular provider. AEO, answer engine optimization, supports how your business can be represented in that kind of research.

The purpose is to give potential customers another way to encounter relevant business information. Your company still needs to be a suitable provider for the work that person needs.

An Essential Part of Customer Acquisition

Referrals can introduce people to your business, but search can reach people who have not received a recommendation. Someone new to the area or looking for a service for the first time may begin online.

Essentials is our starting level of ongoing management. All three levels support the same broad purpose, with increasing amounts of work. The value begins with making your business part of the search for the services it provides.
