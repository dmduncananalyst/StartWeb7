Do reviews help me get more customers from Google?

Yes. Reviews help people evaluate your business, and Google identifies reviews and positive ratings as factors that can support local prominence. Recent feedback about your services can help someone feel comfortable contacting you.

Reviews describe the customer experience

A business can explain what it offers, while reviewers describe what happened when they used it. Someone comparing providers may look for details about communication, the work or reliability. Those details help them decide whether the company fits their own priorities.

A number does not tell the entire story

Reviews give potential customers a view of other people’s experiences. Recent comments about the service, communication and completed work can help someone decide to contact the business.

Local visibility and customer choice differ

Specific reviews can answer practical questions about what it is like to work with your company. Those details support the service information on your website and listing.

Honesty matters

A review should reflect a genuine experience. Google prohibits certain manipulative practices, including incentives for reviews. A business cannot responsibly treat a flattering review as something to purchase. Misleading experiences make the information less useful for the people considering the company.

Reviews do not replace the service

Positive reviews can help someone feel comfortable calling or booking. Your website gives that person a place to explore the work and find the relevant contact option.

A rating is a summary of different experiences

Two customers can give the same rating for different reasons. One may value communication, while another may focus on the finished work or the timing of an appointment. The written explanation can provide context that the average score does not.

Google identifies reviews and positive ratings as factors that can support local prominence. Together with service information, reviews help people discover and evaluate your business.

Relevant reviews can help someone feel more confident about choosing the company. They may recognize a service similar to the one they want and decide to call or arrange an appointment. That confidence can support the move from comparing providers to buying.
