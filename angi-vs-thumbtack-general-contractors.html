CONTRACTOR LEAD SOURCES COMPARED

Contractors need a reliable way to be found by homeowners who are already looking for the work they provide.

The Lead Problem

Contractors often depend on a mix of referrals, repeat work, and outside lead sources. Those can all have a place, but they do not always create a dependable path for new homeowners to find the company directly.

How an Owned Lead Source Helps

A contractor website built around relevant searches gives homeowners a direct path from researching a project to contacting the company.

Why Search Results Change Over Time

Homeowner searches, competing pages, and project questions change over time. Ongoing SEO + AEO management keeps the website useful and relevant as those changes happen, while giving the contractor a direct place to receive inquiries.

The Bottom Line

Over time, a contractor’s website can become a lead source of its own—one built around the company, not someone else’s platform.
