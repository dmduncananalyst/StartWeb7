AEO EXPLAINED

WHAT IS AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It helps a business have a chance to appear in answers from tools like ChatGPT when someone asks about a service they need. That person may then visit the business’s website, call or stop by, and become a customer.

What Does That Look Like?

Imagine someone needs a roof repaired. They ask an AI tool for help finding a local roofer. The tool gives an answer and may mention or link to a business. AEO is about giving a business a chance to be included in answers like that. An AI tool decides what it shows, so a mention is never guaranteed.

How Can AEO Bring More Customers?

The person is already looking for a roofer. If the answer points them to a business and they click through to its website, they arrive as a hot lead, someone with a need who may be ready to hire. They can call or visit the business. If they choose it for the work, they become a customer.

Question, Click, Lead, Customer

Someone asks an AI tool about a service they need. An answer may introduce a business. They click to its website because it seems relevant. That visitor is a hot lead, already looking for help. They can call or visit, and if they hire the business, they become a customer.

Will Every Answer Bring a Customer?

No. An AI answer may leave out the business, give the person enough information without a website visit, or lead them to another choice. AEO creates opportunities to be discovered. It cannot promise a mention, a website visit, or a particular number of customers.

How Is AEO Different From SEO?

SEO helps a business have a chance to appear in Google’s list of websites after someone types in what they need. AEO helps a business have a chance to appear in an answer from an AI tool or answer feature. In both cases, a person who already needs the service may visit the website and decide whether to call or stop by.

What Is the Point of AEO?

The point is to give more people who need a service a way to discover a business and choose it. AEO can create more chances to reach those people over time, but the person decides whom to hire.
