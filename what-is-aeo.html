AEO EXPLAINED

WHAT IS AEO?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It is work that helps a business and its website be understood by tools that answer questions, such as ChatGPT and Google’s AI features. Those answers can introduce a business to people who go on to visit its website, call, or book.

What Does That Look Like?

A person can ask an AI tool a question before knowing which service or company to look for. The answer may explain their options and mention a business or link to its website. That is how someone can discover a service they had not considered.

How Can AEO Bring More Customers?

An AI answer can introduce a service and a business that provides it. A reader who is interested may click through to the website and call to discuss a purchase or booking. AEO connects that early question with a business, even before the person is ready to buy.

Question Click Lead Customer

A question starts the conversation. A link can bring the reader to a website. An interested potential customer is a lead. If that person books or buys, they become a customer. Some readers act right away, while others take time to decide.

Will Every Answer Bring a Customer?

No. An AI tool chooses which sources and businesses to include. Someone may read the answer without clicking a link, or compare several businesses before deciding. A mention is not the same as a customer.

How Is AEO Different From SEO?

SEO focuses on the results people see after typing into a search engine like Google. AEO focuses on answers from AI tools and other answer features. Both can introduce people to a business and lead to website visits, calls, and sales.

What Is the Point of AEO?

AEO connects a business with people asking questions online. It can introduce a service, explain why it is relevant, and direct readers to a business that offers it. The value for the business comes when that interest turns into customers and revenue.
