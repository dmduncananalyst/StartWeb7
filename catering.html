People often research menus, service styles, event sizes, and availability before contacting a caterer. A clear website helps them understand whether your company fits the occasion and gives them a direct path to inquire.

StartWeb7 provides website development and ongoing SEO + AEO management for catering companies nationwide. The work can evolve as your menus, services, and event focus change—without turning the page into a sales pitch.

Can one website present several types of catering?

Yes. Weddings, corporate events, baby showers, and public and private events can each have a clear place while still feeling like one cohesive catering brand.

Can the website help qualify event inquiries?

Yes. An inquiry experience can collect useful basics such as event type, date, guest count, and preferred service style before the first conversation.

Can the site change when menus or services change?

Yes. Ongoing management can keep the information current as seasonal menus, service options, and the types of events you accept evolve.
