All supplied approved paragraphs remain present.

Assisted Living's existing video now fills its hero instead of appearing as a separate media section beneath a photo hero. Existing media assets are retained. No new image generation was needed for this audit.

Added consistent space between the hero introduction and buttons, between hero and body, between body paragraphs and subsequent headings, and inside industry editorial sections.
