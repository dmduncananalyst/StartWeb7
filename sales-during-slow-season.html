HOW DO I GET SALES DURING A SLOW SEASON?

Say your slow season is August. If you start your SEO back in February or March, by the time August rolls around, your SEO will already be working — bringing in organic customers on its own. That's the idea: no more slow season, if you invest in ongoing SEO.

Prepare for Your Slow Season with SEO

SEO takes a few months to build — optimizing your website, creating content, and earning the rankings search engines look for isn't instant. That's exactly why the timing matters: start it a few months before your slow season, and it has time to kick in right as your slow season begins.

Using the August example: starting in February or March gives your SEO roughly four to six months to build. By August, instead of waiting around for business to come back, you're already ranking and already getting found — so instead of a completely dead month, you've got organic customers coming in the whole way through it.

Why This Works

  • SEO needs lead time. It doesn't produce results overnight, so it has to be started well before you need it, not after.
  • It's already running when your slow season starts. Instead of scrambling in August, the work is already done and already bringing in customers.
  • It has to stay consistent, not be a one-time push. A quick round of SEO in February and then nothing won't hold up through August — rankings are maintained by ongoing work, not a single setup. Keep it consistent, and it keeps working straight through your slow season.
  • The result: a lighter slow season. The organic traffic SEO brings in helps offset the drop-off you'd normally see, even if it doesn't erase it completely.

The Bottom Line

If August is your slow season, don't wait until August to think about SEO — start it in February or March. By the time your slow season would normally hit, you've got organic traffic coming in, so instead of just waiting it out, you're bringing in sales the whole way through.
