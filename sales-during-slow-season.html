HOW DO I GET SALES DURING A SLOW SEASON?

To bring in sales during a slow season, contact past customers, offer services that fit the season, and reach new buyers before demand drops. SEO can support future discovery, but it cannot promise sales by a particular month.

Know which weeks slow down

Review past inquiries and sales by month. Identify which services still sell, which customers buy more than once, and how far ahead people book. Use those patterns to set a realistic target for the coming season.

Contact past customers before the lull

Reach out to customers who may need another service, renewal, or seasonal check in. Give them a specific reason to get in touch and a simple booking path. Ask satisfied customers for introductions where it makes sense.

Offer work that fits the season

Highlight services that customers need during quieter months. If you run a promotion, check the margin and capacity before discounting. A useful package or earlier booking window may be better than cutting the price of everything.

Use channels that match your timeline

Email, direct outreach, referrals, and paid campaigns can reach people sooner when the slow period is close. Improving your website and SEO may help future demand, but search rankings and inquiries are never guaranteed by a particular month.

Measure what actually sells

Track where each inquiry came from, whether it became a sale, and what that sale earned. Compare the result with the time and money spent so you can plan the next slow season more accurately.
