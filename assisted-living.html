ASSISTED LIVING QUESTIONS

How can you help our assisted living community get more residents?

Our website design and ongoing SEO + AEO services help families who need assisted living find your community online, click through to your website, and call to arrange a tour. Those visitors are leads because they are already looking for help, and your team helps them decide whether your community is the right place to live.

What is SEO for assisted living?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It helps your community appear on Google when families are looking for assisted living, bringing more people who need your services to your website.

What is AEO for assisted living?

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization. It helps AI tools understand your community and the services it offers, creating another way for families looking for assisted living to discover you.

Can the website help families arrange a tour?

Yes. Families can call your community or use an interactive chat to ask about a tour and share how they would like to be contacted. Your team confirms availability, answers their questions, and schedules the visit.

Is assisted living the same as home health care?

No. Assisted living is a residential setting, while home health care provides services where a person already lives. We keep those services distinct so families understand what your community offers.
