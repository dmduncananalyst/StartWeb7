Santa Clarita’s Industrial and Corporate Business Market

Santa Clarita’s business market includes aerospace and defense manufacturing, medical devices, advanced manufacturing, digital media, information technology and corporate services. Its economy includes businesses selling specialized products and services to other companies, alongside businesses selling directly to residents.

The City’s Economic Development Division identifies these sectors in its Industry Overview. These sectors give Santa Clarita an industrial and corporate economy alongside its shops and household services.

The customers can range from individual residents to businesses purchasing specialized work. A manufacturer selling a component, a professional firm accepting a corporate account and a business booking individual appointments participate in different markets within Santa Clarita.

Specialized Products and Business Accounts

The City’s advanced manufacturing overview includes electronics, engines and industrial machinery. Its medical device and biotechnology section describes a separate group of businesses, while its aerospace section identifies companies involved in that sector.

These sectors involve more specific buying decisions than a general shopping trip. A business buyer may be evaluating a particular product or supplier before starting a conversation. A sale can involve a company account, an order or a continuing relationship.

A specialized manufacturer can sell beyond Santa Clarita, taking orders from businesses elsewhere. A professional firm can also have clients outside the city while maintaining a local base.

Digital Media, Technology and Professional Services

The City identifies film studios and gaming companies within digital media and entertainment. Its information technology overview includes telecommunications, computer design, data processing, hosting and software publishing. Corporate and professional services are also part of the local business mix.

An entertainment project, a technology purchase and a professional account involve different purchasing decisions. A project can have a defined schedule, while an account can involve continuing work. A product purchase can be another separate transaction.

Together, these sectors create a range of business relationships, from individual projects to continuing accounts. They sit alongside the local businesses that residents use for everyday purchases and appointments.
