GENERAL CONTRACTOR SEO + AEO FAQ

Do you target ADU specific searches separately from general contracting?

Yes. ADU searches behave differently from general renovation searches, so we build out targeting for each project type you handle.

How do you handle SEO for a service area that spans multiple cities?

We build dedicated location pages for each city or region you serve, so you can rank across your full service area instead of just one city.

Can AEO help with searches like “best ADU builder near me” on AI tools?

Yes. AEO structures your site’s information so AI tools like ChatGPT and AI Overviews can pull accurate answers about your services and service area when someone asks for a recommendation.

What’s the difference between Essentials and Competitive for a contractor business in a competitive market?

Competitive expands the amount of content, competitor research, and search targeting per month, worth considering if your market has several established contractors already ranking well.
