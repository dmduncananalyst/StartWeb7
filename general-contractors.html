GENERAL CONTRACTORS AND ONLINE DISCOVERY

StartWeb7 provides website design, SEO and AEO for general contractors. Your website can present your construction services, completed work and service area while giving property owners a direct route to request an estimate. SEO + AEO support discovery through Google and AI answers as people explore their next project.

Construction services with a defined scope

A complete estate renovation involves a different conversation from a commercial tenant improvement or a new addition. Your site should describe the projects your company actually accepts, with enough detail for someone to recognize the service they need. That gives the business a more useful identity than a general claim about construction.

Contractors with several specialties can present them as related parts of one company. Additions, remodeling, ADU construction and commercial work can each have their own context when they are part of your offering. Clear service descriptions make the range understandable while keeping the work connected to your company’s experience.

Residential estates and commercial properties

Estate projects can involve substantial living spaces, exterior improvements and several areas of a property working together. The website can show the scale and finish of completed work through project photography and descriptions supplied by the contractor. This gives owners a view of the kind of property and project your team handles.

Commercial clients are looking at a different setting. Offices, retail spaces and other business properties have their own use, access and scheduling considerations. A clear commercial introduction helps an owner or property representative explore that side of the company without reading through descriptions intended only for residential projects.

Let completed work tell a specific story

A project portfolio can show more than a collection of attractive rooms. Captions can identify the type of work, the area completed and the overall scope, giving visitors context for what they are seeing. A full renovation and a single-room update then become recognizable examples of different projects.

SEO + AEO for construction discovery

People search for contractors through the work they want completed and the place where the property is located. SEO supports your visibility in relevant Google results, helping owners discover the company while researching those projects. The website gives them the service and portfolio detail behind that introduction.

AEO adds another route as people use AI tools to explore renovation and construction options. A person may begin by comparing project types before knowing which contractor to contact. Your company’s online presence gives interested readers a place to learn about the actual services and ask about their property.

Present the company behind the projects

Construction clients often want to understand the people and company responsible for the work. Your website can present the business background, project focus and credentials you supply alongside the portfolio. Those details keep the presentation grounded in your company rather than in generic construction language.
