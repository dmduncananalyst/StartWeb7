How do I target the right people with ads?

Target the people looking for the services you offer in the areas you cover. A clear offer and relevant audience help connect your campaign with potential customers. Calls and appointment requests show which services they are interested in.

Reaching the right audience

An advertisement can be shown widely while reaching few people who can use the service. A company serving one region may receive attention from people outside it. A specialist may attract general interest without attracting the kind of work it accepts.

For a service campaign, the audience needs to match the work and location in the offer. That keeps the advertisement relevant to people considering those services.

The service sets the practical boundaries

A major project and an immediate repair involve different needs. Clear offers help connect each campaign with the people looking for that kind of work.

For example, an appointment for an unavailable service will not become useful revenue just because the person clicked an advertisement. The contact may be genuine, but the match is wrong. That is different from the advertisement producing no attention at all.

Platform controls are not certainty

The tools used to define an audience are ways of making choices about distribution. They do not give the advertiser complete knowledge of an individual’s intentions. A person can fit an audience description and still have no intention to buy.

Targeting options help keep a campaign relevant to its offer. Reviewing the services people ask about gives you useful feedback on which audiences are contacting the business.

Quality appears after the advertisement

Calls and appointment requests reveal which services people are interested in. That information adds useful context to the campaign’s audience reporting.

Review campaign activity alongside the service requests recorded by your business. That helps show which audiences are contacting you about the work you offer.

Review the business it brings

The report should explain whether its results are views, website visits, calls, appointments or sales. Each number describes a different point in the customer’s decision. A vague success total can obscure the difference.

The purpose of targeting is to reach people interested in your services. A clear offer and relevant audience give those people a way to discover the business and get in touch.
