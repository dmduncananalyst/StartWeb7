AEO + AI SEARCH

CAN CHATGPT RECOMMEND MY BUSINESS?

Yes, ChatGPT can mention or recommend a business when it has relevant information, especially when it searches for current local options. There is no guaranteed placement, and whether your business appears depends on the question and the information available.

Short Answer: Yes, But It Depends

When ChatGPT searches the web for current information, it can use public information from websites, reviews, directories, and other pages that mention a business. Without web search, an answer is limited to information already available to the model and may not reflect a local business accurately or currently.

For local businesses, the relevant situation is when someone asks ChatGPT to find, compare, or recommend a service in a specific location and it searches the web to answer.

What Can Affect a Recommendation

AI search needs enough clear, credible public information to understand what a business does, where it serves, and why it may be relevant to a person’s question. This can include a business website, public listings, reviews, and other reputable sources.

Nothing guarantees an AI recommendation. Results can change based on the exact question, location, sources used, and information available at that time.

What This Means for Your Business

This is part of Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO: making a business easier for search engines and AI tools to understand when people ask questions about a service, location, or need.

AEO does not replace SEO. The same foundation that helps people find a business through Google can also help AI tools understand that business when they search the web for an answer.

Why Clear Information Must Stay Current

Business details, services, reviews, and customer questions change over time. Ongoing SEO + AEO management helps keep that information clear and current so search engines and AI platforms have accurate public information to work from.

The goal is not to chase a trick or promise an AI recommendation. It is to maintain a reliable online presence wherever customers are searching—on Google, Maps, and AI platforms.

The Bottom Line

ChatGPT can recommend a business when it has current web information to work from. A business that is clear, consistently represented, and easy to understand online has a stronger foundation to be surfaced than one that is difficult to find or describe.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) helps make your website easier for AI search tools to understand, giving your business more opportunities to appear in AI answer boxes and responses when people ask questions in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.
