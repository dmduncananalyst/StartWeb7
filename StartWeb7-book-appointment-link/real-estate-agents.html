REAL ESTATE SEO + AEO FAQ

Do you help with SEO for individual real estate agents or just teams and brokerages?

Both. We work with solo agents, teams, and brokerages, and build out the site and keyword targeting around how you actually generate business.

Can you target more than one city or neighborhood?

Yes. We build dedicated pages for each city or neighborhood you work in, so you can rank for searches across your whole service area instead of just one location.

How is AEO different from SEO for a real estate website?

SEO helps you rank in Google search results. AEO structures your site’s information so AI tools like ChatGPT and AI Overviews can accurately answer questions like “who’s a good real estate agent in [city]” with your business.

Do I need a new website, or can you do SEO on my current site?

Either works. If your current site is solid, we can build SEO + AEO management on top of it. If it needs work, we may recommend starting with a new site first.
