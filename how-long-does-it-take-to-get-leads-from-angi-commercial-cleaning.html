COMMERCIAL CLEANING

HOW LONG DOES IT TAKE TO GET LEADS FROM ANGI?

For a commercial cleaning owner, the honest answer is that it depends on your service area, categories, availability, and how quickly your team responds. Angi can create opportunities quickly in some markets, but it is not an instant, guaranteed flow of the exact contracts you want.

What to expect from Angi

Angi is a marketplace where people request help and businesses can receive opportunities. The time before you receive a lead can vary, and so can the quality of each opportunity. A business owner should look at the service area, the type of cleaning requested, the expected job size, and the cost of each lead before deciding if it is a good fit.

For commercial cleaning, speed is only one part of the decision

A quick request is not automatically a good commercial cleaning contract. Ask whether the inquiry is for recurring service, the kind of facility you want, and a location your team can serve. Track how many conversations become walk throughs, proposals, and signed monthly contracts.

Build a second way for clients to find you

SEO, Search Engine Optimization, helps your own website appear when businesses search Google for commercial cleaning, janitorial service, office cleaning, and porter service. Unlike a marketplace profile, your website can explain your services, facility types, service areas, and process in your own words.

What is AEO?

AEO, Answer Engine Optimization, helps make your website easier for AI search tools to understand, giving your business more opportunities to appear in AI answer boxes and responses when people ask questions in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini.

Use both if the numbers work

There is no rule that says you have to choose only one. Angi can be one source of opportunities while SEO and AEO build a longer term route for potential clients to find your cleaning company directly. The key is measuring the source of each signed contract, not only the number of new inquiries.
