SEO EXPLAINED

WHAT IS SEO?

SEO stands for Search Engine Optimization. It helps a business show up on Google for people who already need its services. Those people can visit the website, call or stop by, and become customers.

What Does That Look Like?

Imagine someone needs a roof repaired. They open Google and type “roof repair near me.” That is a search. Google shows a list of places to visit online. SEO helps a business have a chance to appear in that list without paying for an ad each time a person visits its website.

How Can SEO Bring More Customers?

The person sees a business in Google and clicks its website. They are already looking for that service, so they arrive as a hot lead, someone with a need who may be ready to hire. The website helps them decide whether to call or visit the business. If they choose that business for the work, they become a customer.

Search, Click, Lead, Customer

Someone needs a service and types it into Google. They click a business’s website because it looks relevant. That visitor is a hot lead, already looking for help. They can call or visit the business, and if they hire it, they become a customer. SEO helps more of these people find the business.

Does SEO Guarantee Customers?

No. A person might see a business and choose another, or visit its website without calling or stopping by. SEO can help a business get found by more people who need its services, but it cannot promise a particular Google position or number of customers.

Is SEO the Same as Paying for Ads?

No. Ads are paid placements in Google. SEO is about helping a business appear in the regular list of results. Both can bring people to a website. A person who is already looking for the service has a reason to call or visit.

How Long Does SEO Take?

SEO is ongoing work, and results usually develop over time. The point is to give people who need the service more chances to find the business, visit its website, and choose to become customers.
