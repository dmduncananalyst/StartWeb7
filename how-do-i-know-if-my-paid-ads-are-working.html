PAID ADS

How do I know if my paid ads are working?

Your paid ads should bring in customers and enough sales to justify what you spend. Compare the revenue connected to the ads with your advertising and delivery costs. That gives you a business answer, rather than a report focused only on website activity.

Spending is the beginning of the comparison

An advertising bill shows what the company paid for the placement or activity. It does not show what the company earned. The owner needs both sides of that comparison to judge the result fairly.

Compare the advertising spend with the sales connected to the campaign. Include the cost of delivering those jobs when reviewing the financial return, so the figures reflect your business.

Connect advertising with revenue

Google Ads can report actions such as purchases and phone calls. Clearly labeled measurements show what people did after interacting with an advertisement.

The report should connect the advertising with the paid work it produced. For a service company, that may include appointments, projects or recurring contracts. Revenue and delivery costs give the owner a useful basis for evaluating the spending.

Customer quality changes the value

The services requested matter alongside the number of calls. A report that includes the type of work helps you see which advertisements are attracting relevant leads.

A business handling substantial projects may benefit from a smaller number of appropriate prospects. Another business may depend on frequent smaller purchases. There is no universal activity total that establishes success for both companies.

Time affects the comparison

Some customers buy immediately, while others compare providers or arrange work later. An advertising report and the corresponding business records may therefore cover different points in the decision. That difference should be explained rather than ignored.

A partial month is also not directly comparable with a full month. A change in capacity, cancellations or service mix can affect the result. Fair evaluation uses compatible periods and makes those differences visible.

Ask what the evidence supports

A clear report names the actions measured and the source of its figures. That makes spending, leads and campaign activity easier to review together.

The business objective is more suitable customers and sustainable revenue. An advertisement can produce attention without meeting that objective, or a smaller campaign can contribute valuable work. The strongest assessment connects spending to actual customer outcomes without treating every platform action as a purchase.
