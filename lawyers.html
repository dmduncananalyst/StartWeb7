LAW FIRM SEO + AEO FAQ

How much does SEO cost for a law firm?

It depends on your practice area, market, and goals. Personal injury in a large city is more competitive than estate planning in a smaller market. We start with an audit so you see exactly what is needed before you commit to anything.

Can AI search help potential clients find my firm?

Yes. AI tools can surface law firms when their information is clear and relevant. AEO management improves how your firm is represented in AI answers without guaranteeing a recommendation.

Do you work with solo attorneys as well as larger firms?

Yes. SEO + AEO management can support solo attorneys and larger firms based on their practice areas, markets, and goals.

Do I need a new website, or can you do SEO on my current site?

Either works. If your current site is solid, we can build SEO + AEO management on top of it. If it needs work, we may recommend starting with a new site first.
