MEDICAL SEO + AEO FAQ

How much does SEO cost for a medical practice?

It depends on your specialty, market, and goals. A plastic surgeon in a large city faces more competition than a dermatologist in a smaller market. We start with an audit so you see exactly what is needed before you commit to anything.

Can AI search help patients find my practice?

Yes. AI tools can surface medical practices when their information is clear and relevant. AEO management improves how your practice is represented in AI answers without guaranteeing a recommendation.

Do you work with solo practitioners as well as larger practices?

Yes. SEO + AEO management can support solo practitioners and larger practices based on their specialties, markets, and goals.

Do I need a new website, or can you do SEO on my current site?

Either works. If your current site is solid, we can build SEO + AEO management on top of it. If it needs work, we may recommend starting with a new site first.
