Glendale’s Business Market Is More Than Downtown

Glendale has several commercial districts with different mixes of retail, restaurants, offices and neighborhood services. A store, an automotive business and a professional firm can all be based in Glendale while selling to different customers.

The City’s Economic Development Division identifies Downtown, Montrose Shopping Park, Kenneth Village, Adams Square and Brand Boulevard of Cars. Each represents a different commercial setting, from regional shopping to neighborhood purchases and automotive buying.

A Downtown retailer can attract people planning a shopping trip, while a neighborhood service business can depend on appointments and repeat visits.

Regional Shopping and Corporate Activity

Downtown includes the Americana at Brand and Glendale Galleria, across the street from one another. The City also identifies corporate and technology businesses toward the northern part of Downtown. Retail and corporate activity therefore sit within the same district without representing the same customer market.

A shopper comparing stores is purchasing for themselves. A corporate buyer comparing providers may be purchasing for a company. A restaurant can sell to either group, but a meal for an individual and an order for a business event are different purchases.

Being in Downtown puts a business in a recognizable commercial area. Customers still choose between individual companies based on what they want to buy.

Neighborhood Districts and Automotive Buyers

Montrose Shopping Park includes shops and restaurants in a main street setting. Kenneth Village includes eateries, shops and service providers, while Adams Square is identified as a neighborhood business center. Brand Boulevard of Cars brings another type of buying decision into Glendale’s market.

A neighborhood purchase can involve familiarity and convenience. Buying a vehicle can involve comparing several businesses before making a major purchase. These are different commercial situations, not evidence that one district automatically produces better customers than another.

A neighborhood shop can build repeat business through regular visits. An automotive business may spend more time with each buyer before a sale. Both want more customers, but the purchases happen differently.

From Shopping Plans to Store Visits

People can choose a Glendale business before they leave home, whether they are planning a visit to Downtown, comparing a vehicle purchase or booking an appointment in their neighborhood.
