Connect Your Website to Customer Searches

People are searching for the services your business provides, often before they know your company name. StartWeb7 combines website design with SEO and AEO optimization to help your site reach those potential customers. The purpose is a website that supports both search traffic and customer inquiries.

Reach Beyond People Who Already Know You

A website can serve people arriving from referrals or links you share. Search offers another opportunity, reaching people who are actively researching the kind of work you provide without already knowing your name.

An SEO optimized website connects its business purpose with that search activity. The emphasis is relevant people looking for your services, with a website where they can learn about your company and contact it.

Make Search Traffic Useful to Your Business

A visitor arriving from a search needs information relevant to the service they were researching. The website should help them understand the work you handle, where you operate and how to make an inquiry.

If search visibility is not producing inquiries, explore why your business may not be getting leads from Google and why your website may not be bringing in customers.

That is why design and optimization belong together. Attracting attention has a business purpose when the person who arrives can recognize the service they need and reach your team about it.

Website Design and Search Optimization Together

This option combines the website project with SEO and AEO optimization. SEO supports a website’s presence in search results, while AEO addresses how business information can be understood in AI search experiences.

Ongoing SEO and AEO management is available separately through Essentials, Competitive and Dominance. The website project creates the site, while ongoing management continues the search work as your business and its market develop.
