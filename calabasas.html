Calabasas Has Several Commercial Settings

Calabasas combines historic commercial space, shopping centers, offices and business park uses. Its business market includes both purchases made at a destination and services arranged with a company before a customer visits or books.

The City’s commercial zoning descriptions distinguish office, mixed use, business park and Old Town settings. They accommodate businesses ranging from shops and professional firms to companies selling to other businesses.

A shop can depend on customer visits, an office can see clients by appointment and a supplier can take orders from other companies. Calabasas has space for each of those business models.

Old Town and Destination Businesses

The City Profile identifies the Leonis Adobe as central to Old Town’s historic context. The Old Town commercial district accommodates office, retail and other commercial uses within a setting governed by historic preservation requirements.

A historic setting gives a district a recognizable identity, but it does not tell a buyer what each company sells. An owner still has to distinguish a restaurant visit, a retail purchase or a professional appointment. Those are separate reasons to choose a business in the same area.

Old Town’s identity can be part of the experience for a destination business. A professional firm in the same area may be chosen for its services rather than for the setting around its office.

Shopping Centers, Offices and Business Parks

The City’s development resources identify commercial settings including The Commons, Las Virgenes Village and Calabasas Park/The Commons Office. Its zoning descriptions also recognize business park uses, including offices and light industrial activity.

That broadens the market beyond the image of Calabasas as a residential community. Some purchases happen at a shopping destination, while others are professional services or business orders arranged in advance.

For a company selling an ongoing service, the meaningful outcome may be a new client account. For a retailer, it may be a purchase during a visit. A shopping trip and a professional appointment bring customers into Calabasas businesses in different ways.

More Visits and Client Appointments

A customer planning a shopping trip to The Commons and someone comparing professional services may both use Google before choosing a business. One purchase leads to a store visit, while another begins with a phone call and an appointment. SEO helps your company appear in relevant unpaid results while that decision is being made.
